01 Is NEXLOOP a real AI product? +

No. NEXLOOP is an independent frontend concept created for a development portfolio. Its interface, workflows, and pricing are illustrative.

02 Does the workflow demo use AI? +

The interactive demo uses predefined responses in JavaScript. It shows how a real AI integration could feel, without connecting to an external API.

03 Does the pricing toggle work? +

Yes. The toggle updates the displayed example prices. There is no checkout or payment processing.

04 Is this landing page mobile-friendly? +

Yes. Its layout adapts to smaller devices, including navigation, dashboard, feature cards, and pricing.
